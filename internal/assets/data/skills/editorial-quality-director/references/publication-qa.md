# Publication QA gate

Use only for deep-edit/publication tasks or when the user explicitly requests a final editorial audit.

## Gate 1 — fidelity

- Thesis/intent preserved.
- Required details retained.
- No new unsupported claims.
- No accidental certainty inflation.
- Quotes/citations remain faithful.
- Protected technical tokens intact.

## Gate 2 — architecture

- Opening earns its place.
- Section order follows reader needs.
- Headings match section content.
- Paragraphs each have a clear function.
- Repetition is intentional or removed.
- Conclusion closes the task rather than merely repeating it.

## Gate 3 — prose

- Sentences are readable at first pass.
- Rhythm is not monotonous.
- Pronouns have clear antecedents.
- Transitions express real relationships.
- Filler is removed.
- Specific language replaces vague abstraction when source supports it.

## Gate 4 — anti-slop

- No canned grandiosity.
- No empty "importance" language.
- No artificial symmetry used as a substitute for reasoning.
- No fake quotes/attribution.
- No generic conclusion boilerplate.
- No unnecessary meta-commentary.
- No overcorrection that erased author voice.

## Gate 5 — mechanics

- Grammar.
- Spelling.
- Punctuation.
- Capitalization.
- Quote/dash conventions.
- Lists.
- Heading levels.
- Units/numbers.
- Dates.
- Abbreviations.

## Gate 6 — regression

Compare source and final.

Explicitly sample-check:

- every numeric cluster;
- every proper noun;
- every link;
- every code/command block;
- every citation marker;
- every heading;
- every explicit requirement/prohibition.

## Gate 7 — destination fit

Ask internally:

- Would the intended reader understand what to do/think next?
- Is the level of formality appropriate?
- Is the text denser or longer than its job requires?
- Did editing make it sound generic?
- Is anything "polished" at the expense of specificity?

Only call the text publication-ready when all applicable gates pass.
